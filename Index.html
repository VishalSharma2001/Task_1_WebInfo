イベントレポート / Event Report

EcoFood
was featured at an event in Kawasaki City!

We are advancing a new initiative in collaboration with Kawasaki City aimed at reducing food waste. The food waste reduction app “EcoFood” was showcased and promoted at a Kawasaki City event held from Friday, October 30, to Sunday, November 1, 2026.
At this event, posters and materials introducing EcoFood were displayed at the venue in Kawasaki City, providing an opportunity for many local residents to learn about EcoFood’s initiatives.
EcoFood is a platform dedicated to reducing food waste by connecting surplus, yet perfectly edible, food—generated by restaurants, supermarkets, bakeries, and similar businesses—with those who need it.
By leveraging the power of technology, we aim to create a society where food is not wasted, and where everyone can enjoy delicious meals at an affordable price.
Taking this initiative in Kawasaki City as a significant first step, we will work together with local residents, restaurants, and businesses to expand the movement toward reducing food waste.Toward a future where edible food is never thrown away.
EcoFood is expanding a sustainable system for reducing food waste—starting from Kawasaki City, reaching across Japan, and extending to the rest of the world.。
